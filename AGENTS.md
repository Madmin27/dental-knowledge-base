# Dental Open Source implementation and review

Read [the independent-review policy](docs/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) before substantial
implementation or review work. Follow `/root/sunucu/AGENTS.md` for server work.

- After each substantial milestone, add a dated handoff under `docs/codex/` using
  `REVIEW-HANDOFF-TEMPLATE.md`. Pin the reviewed commit and distinguish local
  tests, CI, deployment, real-device feedback and qualified human acceptance.
- Never overwrite an earlier review snapshot to imply it covered later changes.
- The implementation agent may fix findings and supply evidence. It must not
  close independent findings solely on its own assertion or claim independent
  approval of its own changes. Human maintainers decide priority and closure.
- Keep private contribution records, tracking tokens, credentials and clinical
  material out of handoffs, GitHub issues and external review prompts.
- Academic/anatomical findings require recorded qualified human review. AI
  critique, votes among models, CI and public HTTPS do not provide that approval.
- GitHub review issues concern public implementation/source-presentation defects;
  participant contributions and academic decisions remain on the platform.
- Owner priority: provide a realistic source-based preview first. After the owner
  records handover to the qualified expert team, stop autonomous AI anatomical
  proposals/edits/review; anatomy belongs to that team. Further anatomical AI
  assistance requires their explicit request. Separately authorized technical
  maintenance does not confer anatomical authority.
